Privacy Policy

Plazio – seating planner app · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short

Plazio does not collect, process or transmit any personal data to the developer or to third parties. Everything you enter stays on your device.

2. What data does the app handle?

In the app you enter names of guests or students, rules, tables and plans. This data is stored only locally on your device (the app's local database). It is not sent to us or to any server, and we have no access to it.

Deleting a plan or uninstalling the app removes the locally stored data. Backups made by your operating system itself (for example device backups via iCloud or Google) are governed by your settings and by Apple's or Google's privacy terms.

3. No account, no analytics, no ads

  • There is no user account and no registration.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking services.
  • The app shows no ads and includes no advertising networks.
  • The app does not need an internet connection for its features.
  • The app does not request permissions such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.

4. PDF export and sharing

When you create a PDF, it is generated on your device. Whether and with whom you share it (for example by printing, email or messenger) is entirely up to you via your operating system's share function. The terms of the app you choose apply to that sharing.

5. In-app purchases

The full version can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was made. We never see your payment details.

6. Note for teachers

Because no data leaves the app, student names are not sent to the developer or to any cloud service by the app. Please still check your school's or education authority's rules on handling student data on private devices.

7. Your rights

Since we do not store any personal data about you, we cannot provide information or delete data on our side. You have full control over the data on your device at all times. If you have questions, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub technically processes connection data (for example your IP address), which we cannot influence. We ourselves use no cookies, no analytics and no external resources.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that requires an update to this policy, we will update it and show the new date at the top.
